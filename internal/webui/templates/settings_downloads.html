Downloads

How Coldarr treats qBittorrent's downloads on your downloads tiers. A finished download sits on the download drive for the settle time, then moves to seed storage and gets the seed label. Downloads still in progress are never moved, and neither is anything without a label set to move.

Moving

How long a finished download stays on the download drive before it moves - time for Radarr/Sonarr/Lidarr to import it.

{{range .Categories}}

The category a download gets once it has landed on seed storage - only then, so a move that fails never leaves a download labeled as moved. Coldarr creates it in qBittorrent if it's missing.

qBittorrent keeps a download with Automatic Torrent Management on in its category's folder, so it would undo a move. Left unticked, such downloads stay put and show on the Downloads page.

Labels

What Coldarr does with downloads carrying each of qBittorrent's categories and tags. A download moves only if one of its labels says to, and never if one says to leave it alone. A label with no rule - including one you add in qBittorrent later - is left alone until you give it one. Pick the app that imports a label's downloads, and Coldarr waits until that app has imported each download before moving it.
