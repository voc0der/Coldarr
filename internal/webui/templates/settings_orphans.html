Orphaned Storage

Folders sitting on a configured tier path that no longer correspond to anything Radarr, Sonarr, Lidarr, Jellyfin, or qBittorrent still tracks - a deleted movie/series whose folder was never cleaned up, a failed import, a leftover from a prior reorganization. Something known to any one of those services is not an orphan, even if the others have never heard of it.
