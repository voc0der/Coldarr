Tiers

A tier is a named policy applied to one or more physical paths. Hot storage is never steered toward a usage level - every cold-eligible item on it is a move candidate whenever cold tiers have room. Cold tiers get packed toward their target, falling back to their max as a last resort, never crossing it. If two paths (even across different tiers) turn out to be the same physical volume, Coldarr detects that automatically and treats their capacity as shared - see "Shares volume with" below.
